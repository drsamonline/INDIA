This atlas stands on other people's work: the Wikipedia editors who wrote the histories, the photographers who released their pictures, the type designers, and the three.js contributors. Everything is named here.

1 · The models

All 24 3D models are generated at runtime from code — no downloaded meshes, no photogrammetry, no image textures. They are written by Dr Sohil Momin and released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. You may copy, modify, redistribute or sell them without asking permission or giving credit (though a link back is always welcome).

3 · Text

The history, dates and figures on each monument page are adapted from the English Wikipedia article for that monument, which is published under CC BY-SA 4.0. The adapted text on this site is offered under the same licence. Each page links directly to its source article.

  • Taj Mahal · Qutb Minar · Hawa Mahal · Charminar
  • Gateway of India · India Gate · Mysore Palace · Golden Temple
  • Konark Sun Temple · Sanchi · Victoria Memorial · Meenakshi Temple

UNESCO World Heritage records: Taj Mahal (252), Qutb Minar (233), Konark (246), Sanchi (524), Jaipur City (1605).

4 · Software

  • three.js r169 — MIT licence. The renderer, the physical sky, the water and the post-processing passes all come from it.
  • OrbitControls, Sky, Water, EffectComposer, UnrealBloomPass, OutputPass and BufferGeometryUtils are part of three.js and are vendored in assets/vendor/three.
  • No other JavaScript libraries are used. The site has no build step and runs from any static file server.

5 · Typefaces

Three typefaces are self-hosted as WOFF2, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:

  • Cormorant Garamond by Christian Thalmann — display serif.
  • JetBrains Mono by JetBrains — interface and labels.
  • Noto Sans Devanagari by Google — Devanagari names of the monuments.

6 · Third-party notice

Every work bundled here that is not original to this repository is listed with its licence in NOTICE: the eighteen photographs, the Wikipedia text, three.js and the three typefaces. One photograph, the Gateway of India by A.Savin, is under the Free Art Licence rather than a Creative Commons licence; NOTICE says so and links the licence text.

7 · Corrections

If a date, a dimension or an attribution is wrong, please open an issue on GitHub. Corrections to the history are best made on Wikipedia first, where the source article is maintained.
